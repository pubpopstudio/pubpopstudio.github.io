FAQ

Which macOS versions are supported?

PSTextEdit requires macOS 13 (Ventura) or later.

How does auto-backup work?

PSTextEdit continuously saves a backup of your work in the background. Your original files are never overwritten — they are only updated when you explicitly press ⌘S.

Can I restore my previous session?

Yes. PSTextEdit automatically restores your previously open files and tabs when you relaunch the app.

What programming languages are supported?

PSTextEdit supports syntax highlighting for 20+ languages including C, C++, Python, JavaScript, TypeScript, Swift, Go, Rust, Java, Kotlin, Ruby, PHP, SQL, HTML, CSS, Shell, and more.

What file encodings are supported?

UTF-8, UTF-8 with BOM, ANSI (Windows-1252), UCS-2 Big Endian, and UCS-2 Little Endian.

How do I report a bug?

Please send an email to pilsun.jang@gmail.com with a description of the issue, steps to reproduce it, and your macOS version. Screenshots are always helpful!
