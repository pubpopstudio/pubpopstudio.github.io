Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 13, 2026

Overview

PSTextEdit is a text and code editor developed by PubPop Studio. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how PSTextEdit handles your data.

Data Collection

PSTextEdit does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. The app operates entirely on your device and does not connect to any external servers.

Files and Documents

All files you open and edit in PSTextEdit remain on your device. The app accesses files only when you explicitly open them or when restoring your previous session. Auto-backup files are stored locally on your device and are never uploaded anywhere.

Analytics and Tracking

PSTextEdit does not use any analytics, tracking, or advertising frameworks. No usage data is collected or transmitted.

In-App Purchases

PSTextEdit offers optional tip jar purchases to support the developer. These transactions are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. PubPop Studio does not receive or store any payment or financial information.

Third-Party Services

PSTextEdit does not integrate with any third-party services, APIs, or SDKs that collect user data.

Children's Privacy

PSTextEdit does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us at pilsun.jang@gmail.com.
